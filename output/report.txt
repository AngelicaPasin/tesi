# Aquaculture Monitoring Report

## 1. Executive Summary
The fish farming facility experienced significant mortality and environmental challenges over the past week. Key issues include 3200 mortalities, behavioral anomalies on four days, and critical water quality parameters in yellow or red alert levels.

## 2. Fish Health & Behavior
- **Mortality**: Total of 3200 deaths (400 per day average), with a mortality rate of 15.43 per thousand.
- **Appetite**: Stable at a mean value of 1.0.
- **Behavioral Anomalies**: Observed on four days, indicating potential stress or environmental issues.
- **Symptoms**: Notable issues include exophthalmos in 4 fish and hemorrhages in 5.

## 3. Environmental & Meteorological Conditions
- **Oxygen (5.39 mg/L)**: Yellow alert, near lower threshold for fish health.
- **Temperature (26.31°C)**: Yellow alert, above optimal range for some species.
- **Turbidity (219.15 NTU)**: Red alert, potentially harmful to fish gills and vision.
- **Meteorology**: Stable with decreasing winds and no significant rain; sea surface temperature at 26.29°C (yellow alert).

## 4. Critical Issues & Sensor Anomalies
- High mortality rates and behavioral changes indicate potential stress or disease.
- Environmental parameters in yellow/red alerts suggest water quality issues.
- No sensor inconsistencies detected.

## 5. Recommended Corrective Actions
1. **Water Quality Management**: Address high turbidity with improved circulation or treatment of incoming water.
2. **Oxygenation**: Increase aeration to maintain safe oxygen levels.
3. **Temperature Mitigation**: Consider shading or cooling strategies to lower water temperature.
4. **Behavior Monitoring**: Closely monitor for stressors and consider environmental adjustments.
5. **Health Check**: Investigate potential diseases contributing to mortality.

## 6. Conclusions
The facility faces moderate to high risk due to turbidity levels and mortality rates. Immediate action is necessary to mitigate these issues and prevent further decline in fish health.
